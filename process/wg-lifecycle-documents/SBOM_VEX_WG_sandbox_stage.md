## 2. Mission and Scope Alignment

### Mission

The mission of the SBOM & VEX Working Group is to drive the practical
adoption, harmonization, and maturity of Software Bills of Materials
(SBOM) and Vulnerability Exploitability eXchange (VEX) artifactsacross the open-source ecosystem.

Operating as a format-agnostic, neutral forum under the Open Source Security Foundation (OpenSSF), the WG unites tool builders, software producers, end-users, and standards bodies to bridge the gap between specification and application—transforming community energy into actionable guidance, interoperable tooling, and automated supply chain security outcomes.

### OpenSSF Alignment & Scope

The Working Group structures its scope around three main pillars:

1. **Formats and Standards:** Serving as neutral ground to map
implementation gaps, promote format-neutral advocacy, deliver
implementation guidance for SPDX and CycloneDX, advance VEX
implementations, and bridge external standards bodies (SPDX,
CycloneDX, OASIS CSAF).
2. **Technical Initiatives:** Incubating, hosting, and supporting
developer-focused utilities that automate generation, ingestion,
validation, and enrichment of SBOM and VEX data. Core hosted tools
include **Protobom**, **bomctl**, **SBOMit**, and **OpenVEX**,
alongside domain-specific tools evaluating AI/Data transparency
(e.g., BOMHort, AIkaBoOM).
3. **Education and Adoption:** Lowering deployment barriers through
actionable playbooks, tool benchmarking criteria, and peer-to-peer
community enablement.

### Strategic Cross-Pollination

The WG actively coordinates internally across OpenSSF initiatives:

* **Supply Chain Integrity WG:** Integrating SBOM/VEX artifacts with secure software provenance pipelines.  
* **Vulnerability Disclosures WG:** Aligning VEX ingestion with advisory and vulnerability disclosure practices.
* **Global Cyber Policy WG:** Translating global transparency regulations into developer tools.
* **AI/ML Security WG:** Extending SBOM paradigms to datasets and machine learning models (AIBOM).

## 3. IP Policy and Licensing Due Diligence

* **Code Licensing:** All hosted software projects, tools, and
code contributions adhere strictly to the OpenSSF default
Apache-2.0 license.  
* **Documentation Licensing:** All guides, playbooks, specifications, and educational materials are published under CC-BY-4.0.  
* **Governance & Antitrust:** All members and activities operate under the standard OpenSSF Code of Conduct and Linux Foundation Antitrust Policy.  
* **Contributor Agreements:** Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-offs are required across all code repository contributions.

### Mission of the Working Group

The mission of the OpenSSF Japan Working Group is to serve as a regional coordination and engagement hub that connects Japan’s public sector, industry, academia, developer communities, and the global OpenSSF community. The WG will support awareness, education, localization, community engagement, and feedback activities in Japan related to open source software security and software supply chain security.

The WG addresses an unfulfilled need by providing a sustained Japan-focused structure for collaboration. Cybersecurity and software supply chain security priorities, regulations, and adoption models often vary by country and are influenced by local policy, language, and ecosystem needs. In Japan, effective engagement requires local language support, contextual understanding, and trusted coordination with domestic stakeholders.

The Japan WG does not intend to duplicate the work of existing OpenSSF Working Groups or SIGs. Instead, it will help make OpenSSF activities, best practices, educational materials, and community discussions more accessible in Japan, while also providing a channel for sharing Japan-specific questions, feedback, and observations with relevant OpenSSF global initiatives 

Through this two-way collaboration, the Japan WG aims to strengthen software supply chain security awareness and engagement in Japan while contributing Japan’s perspectives, experience, and participation back to the broader OpenSSF ecosystem.


### Alignment with the OpenSSF MVSSR

1. Catalyst for Change
The Japan WG will help improve awareness and understanding of OpenSSF projects, tools, best practices, and guidance in Japan by serving as a bridge between participants from Japan’s public sector, industry, academia, developer communities, and the global OpenSSF community. The WG may also share Japan-specific questions, feedback, and observations with relevant OpenSSF initiatives, helping OpenSSF better understand local needs and practical challenges in Japan.

2. Educate and Empower the Modern Developer
The Japan WG will support education, awareness, and knowledge sharing in Japan by sharing information about OpenSSF projects, tools, best practices, and educational materials. This includes translation and localization of OpenSSF documentation and training materials, as well as sharing presentations, summaries, and learning materials through Japan community activities.

3. Ecosystem Leader
The Japan WG will support OpenSSF’s engagement with Japan’s public sector, industry, academia, and developer communities by serving as a regional coordination and communication bridge. The WG will help connect Japan-based activities with the broader OpenSSF ecosystem and share local feedback, questions, and observations with relevant OpenSSF global initiatives.

### Pillars: *i) Programs & Projects, ii) Education, iii) Public Policy, iv) Community & Events*
  
1. Programs & Projects
(N/A)

2. Education
The Japan WG will support education, awareness, and knowledge sharing in Japan by localizing and disseminating OpenSSF documentation, training materials, and related guidance. The WG may also share presentations, summaries, and learning materials related to OpenSSF projects and secure software development through Japan community activities.

3. Public Policy
The Japan WG will share global cybersecurity and software supply chain security trends, such as the Cyber Resilience Act, with participants from Japan’s public sector, industry, academia, and developer communities through the Security Meetup. The WG may also share informational feedback with relevant OpenSSF global initiatives, such as common questions, practical concerns, or observations raised through these discussions. Such feedback is intended to be non-binding and will not represent official policy positions or regulatory interpretations.

4. Community & Events
The Japan WG will support community building and outreach in Japan through OpenSSF-related community activities such as the Security Meetup, workshops, conference tracks, and participation in relevant Linux Foundation or OpenSSF-related events. These activities will help share OpenSSF-related information with Japan’s public sector, industry, academia, and developer communities, and provide a channel for connecting the Japan community with the broader OpenSSF ecosystem.

### Community growth plan
Ortelius already has a strong and diverse community, supported by an active Outreach Committee focused on the continued growth and engagement of contributors. The committee works to expand participation across the project, with particular attention to creating opportunities for women and underrepresented minorities to contribute, grow into leadership roles, and become long-term members of the community. Consistent with OpenSSF community-building guidance, Ortelius will continue engaging with adjacent open source communities, technical initiatives, industry practitioners, and organizations that can bring new perspectives and strengthen project resilience. The project also maintains a robust contributor badging program that recognizes participation and provides visible milestones as contributors deepen their involvement. Ortelius is now extending that model with an Adopter Badging Program designed to identify, recognize, and engage organizations and individuals actively using Ortelius. By growing both the contributor and end-user communities, Ortelius can strengthen the feedback loop between those building the project and those deploying it in real-world environments,helping guide priorities, develop new maintainers, document adoption, and demonstrate a broad and active open source ecosystem

### Mission of the project
The mission of Ortelius is to close the gap between vulnerability disclosure and operational remediation by providing continuous, deployment-aware visibility into vulnerabilities affecting software that is actually running. Ortelius maintains a continuously updated digital twin of operational environments by correlating SBOMs, dependency metadata, deployment evidence, runtime endpoints, and vulnerability intelligence such as OSV.dev. This gives organizations the ability to determine which newly disclosed vulnerabilities impact live systems, understand their operational blast radius, identify affected endpoints, and prioritize remediation. Ortelius complements existing OpenSSF initiatives focused on vulnerability disclosure, SBOMs, and software supply chain security by addressing the largely unmet post-deployment need: turning vulnerability disclosures into actionable operational intelligence that reduces the time between vulnerability publication, detection, and remediation.

### Alignment with the OpenSSF MVSSR

Strategies: Ortelius aligns with all three OpenSSF strategic areas and contributes across the four foundational pillars. As a Catalyst for Change, Ortelius provides open source tooling that extends software security beyond development and vulnerability disclosure into post-deployment operations, correlating SBOMs, deployment evidence, runtime endpoints, and continuously updated vulnerability intelligence to help users determine which disclosed vulnerabilities actually affect deployed software. Under Educate & Empower the Modern Developer, Ortelius provides practical tooling, documentation, implementation patterns, and operational visibility that help developers, maintainers, and consumers understand how vulnerabilities propagate from open source components into deployed applications and how those vulnerabilities can be prioritized for remediation. As an Ecosystem Leader, Ortelius provides a deployment-aware perspective that complements OpenSSF initiatives around vulnerability disclosure, SBOMs, software supply chain security, and vulnerability response, while creating opportunities for collaboration on standards and best practices for post-deployment vulnerability management.

Pillars: Ortelius also contributes to OpenSSF's four foundational pillars. Under Programs & Projects, Ortelius provides an actively maintained open source implementation for continuous vulnerability detection, deployment correlation, and remediation workflows. Under Education, the project develops documentation, demonstrations, and best practices that help the community understand SBOM consumption, vulnerability correlation, and post-deployment security. Under Community & Events, Ortelius maintains an active and diverse contributor community, public community meetings, outreach programs, contributor recognition and badging, and a developing adopter program intended to expand participation by end users. Under Policy & Public Sector Engagement, Ortelius can provide practical implementation experience around vulnerability transparency, SBOM consumption, exploitable vulnerability identification, and emerging software-security requirements such as those associated with the EU Cyber Resilience Act. Together, these capabilities support the OpenSSF mission of making it easier to sustainably secure the development, maintenance, release, and consumption of open source software.

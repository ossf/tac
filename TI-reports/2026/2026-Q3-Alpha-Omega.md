# 2026 Q3 Alpha-Omega

## Overview

Alpha-Omega continues "turning money into security", with an increased focus on the Security Engineer in Residence
(SEIR) program, which has seen outstanding success over the past few months.

Our public tools continue to see increasing adoption: Scrutineer (vulnerability analysis, patching, and disclosure),
Threat-Model (AI generated threat models), and Hyrum (AI testcase generation for dependencies).

Alpha-Omega held our annual board meeting last week, focused on strategic planning.

We recently switched to a season-based grant model, and plan to provide more guidance on soliciting grants in particular
areas going forward.

Alpha-Omega has strong interest in helping open source package registries become more financially sustainable through
strategic investment in security features that catalyze feasible business models. We continue to have regular conversations
with package registries and are actively soliciting grant proposals.

## Welcome Calls

### Purpose

Our Welcome Calls are a monthly event in which we highlight maintainers and/or contributors from OpenSSF Projects or working groups to talk about their initiative. The goal is to allow for information sharing for new members to understand the initiative, current efforts, where the TI needs help, and how to connect. Each month, we aim to host diverse TIs to help spread the awareness of OpenSSF WGs and Projects.

## OpenSSF Mentorship Program

### Purpose

Hands-on experience and contributions to OSS projects are a major advantage for obtaining a job in SWE and/or cybersecurity. At the same time, mentoring and coaching experiences are increasingly viewed as important leadership skills in tech jobs. The OpenSSF Mentorship Program (via LFX) offers these experiences and opportunities to students and individuals from underrepresented groups to boost the skills they need to enter the cybersecurity workforce, and to OpenSSF project maintainers wishing to grow their community by mentoring rising developers.

### Current Status

* The Mentorship concluded on August 21 with all 8 mentees successfully completing their projects
* Mentorship Showcase on August 27 featured 4 mentees across 3 projects ([recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kbJ0QvNTr20))

## Proposal: OpenSSF LatAm SIG

### Purpose

Local open source communities in Mexico, Central and South America, and the Caribbean, are thriving but are often overlooked in global conversations about software, AI and supply chain security. The OpenSSF Latin America (LatAm) SIG has two major focus areas. First, the SIG seeks to create pathways for these local efforts to participate at a global level, while respecting the diverse language and cultural needs of different Latin American communities. Second, the SIG aims to bolster local efforts by increasing access to open source and cyber security education, mentorship and collaboration.

### Current Status

This SIG is in the inception phase and hopes to engage current and new OpenSSF members with ties to the broader Latin America region. Activities are planned to start in early 2027.
